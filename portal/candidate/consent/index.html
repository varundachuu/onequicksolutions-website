How We Use Candidate Data

Your profile should support hiring in a clear, limited, and job-focused way.

Job Matching

We use your profile details to identify roles that fit your experience, skills, education, location, and job preferences.

Client Submission Review

Your profile may be reviewed internally by our recruitment team before it is considered for relevant company requirements and shortlist decisions.

Recruitment Communication

We may contact you about shortlisted roles, interviews, profile updates, or information needed to continue your application.

Interview Coordination

We may use your profile and contact details to arrange interview slots, confirmations, reminders, and follow-up related to your application.

Profile Management

Your data helps us maintain a usable candidate profile so you can stay ready for current and future job opportunities.

Support and Updates

We may use your stored details when you ask us to update your profile, correct information, or help you continue with a role already in progress.

Storage, Access, and Your Choice

What this approval means for your data going forward.

Where It Is Stored

Your candidate consent and candidate account details are stored in our application database so the recruitment system can identify approved candidate profiles.

Who Can Access It

Access is intended only for authorized recruitment operations involved in managing candidate profiles, job opportunities, and hiring coordination.

How Long It May Be Kept

Your information may remain available for current openings and future job opportunities unless you ask us to remove or update it, subject to ongoing recruitment needs.

Your Right To Decline

If you do not agree, you may decline now. In that case, the candidate application area will not open and your session will end immediately.

Your Right To Update

If any of your contact, education, skill, or experience details change, you can request updates so your candidate record stays accurate.

Support Contact

For consent questions, profile correction, or removal requests, contact OneQuickSolutions at onequicksolutionsinfo@gmail.com.
